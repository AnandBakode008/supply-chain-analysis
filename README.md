# Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard

A data analytics project built in Power BI analyzing supply chain operations 
across 10,000 SKUs in the Indian market.

## 📊 Dataset Overview
- **Records:** 10,000 product entries
- **Product Categories:** Health Care, Skin Care, Cosmetics, Cloths, Yogamats, HomeGoods
- **Geographic Coverage:** 47 cities across India (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, etc.)
- **Suppliers:** 25 supplier entities (Vertex, Nova, Apex, Global, Prime groups)
- **Shipping Carriers:** 10 carriers including Blue Dart, DHL Express, FedEx, Delhivery, India Post
- **Transportation Modes:** Rail, Road, Sea, Air

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas
- Revenue and pricing trends by product type and region
- Inventory management: stock levels, availability, and order quantities
- Supplier performance: lead times, manufacturing costs, defect rates
- Logistics: shipping costs, carrier performance, transportation mode efficiency
- Quality control: inspection results and defect analysis
- Demand forecasting indicators via production volumes and supply dates

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI** (.pbix) — interactive dashboards and visuals
- **CSV** — raw dataset (10,000 rows × 23 columns)
